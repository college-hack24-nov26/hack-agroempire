# Staff QA Engineer Skill (Krushi Drushti)

Verifies crop scanning algorithms, weather telemetry bounds, and cross-browser responsiveness.

## Test Scenarios
- **Low-light & Blurry Images**: Validates that the AI scanner gracefully warns the user rather than returning a false diagnosis.
- **Offline Transitions**: Disconnect simulation to ensure cached Mandi rates and pre-loaded crop advisories continue to display.
- **Viewport Extremes**: Form factors from 360px budget Android phones to 4K desktop monitor dashboards.
